A CLEAR, SIMPLE POLICY

Privacy & site policy.

This page explains what the Stackwise learning roadmap does when you visit and what to know about services used to display it.

Information this site collects

Stackwise is a static learning resource and does not provide user accounts or collect personal details through these pages. Search terms you type into the roadmap filter are used in your browser to filter the displayed topics; they are not sent to Stackwise by the site code.

The website itself does not include analytics or advertising trackers. The company hosting the site may process basic technical information, such as server request and security logs, under its own policies.

Third-party services

The pages load fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those font files, Google may receive technical request information such as your IP address and browser details. Review Google’s Privacy Policy for information about its practices. You can use browser settings or extensions to block external font requests; the site will fall back to locally available sans-serif and monospace fonts.

Cookies and local storage

The Stackwise pages do not set cookies or store personal information in local storage. Your browser may keep ordinary cached files to load the site more efficiently.

Links to other sites

Some learning materials may link to websites that Stackwise does not operate. Those sites have their own content and privacy practices; review their policies before sharing information with them.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the site’s features or services change. The latest version will be available on this page. Stackwise does not currently provide a contact form on this site.
